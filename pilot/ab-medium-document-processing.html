Quantitative model

This example declares 3 quantities (a budget, checked against a cap). Mermaid has no construct for this — a budget table is not a graph; pie has no cap line, xychart-beta plots series. The shipped budget projection (render/budget.ts) stays bespoke under any adoption of Mermaid as the graph painter.
