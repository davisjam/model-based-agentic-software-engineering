A theory earns credibility only when it explains a real engineering system. This Part follows the
construction of DocAble — a production document-accessibility platform built almost entirely by
directing a fleet of coding agents — from conception through deployment. Not because document
accessibility is uniquely important, but because one deeply observed system reveals engineering
mechanisms that isolated examples cannot.

<!-- thesisbox -->
> ### THE CASE STUDY
>
> Theory predicts engineering.
>
> Engineering tests theory.
>
> Each refines the other.

Watch the two theses become concrete. Models appear because reasoning demands them; mechanisms appear
because failures demand them. The chapters trace the system, its timeline, its architecture, and the
road by which the method emerged — and a closing chapter dwells on the failures, because the dead ends
taught as much as the successes. Over time those failures become engineering capital, and a governed
environment grows out of practice rather than prescription.

In Part IV the industrial organizations served as portability demonstrations: proof that the moves
travel. Here the direction reverses. DocAble is not merely an example of MAGE — it is the deep
originating case, the investigation from which MAGE was induced.
