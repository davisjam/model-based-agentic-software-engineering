book/render_citations.py uses Pandoc citeproc with this local style, so builds do
not download styles or depend on an implicit/default citation style. The JSON
stamp records the CSL SHA-256; updating the style requires regenerating citations.
Print LaTeX uses the same canonical references.bib with its own bibliography style.
