\chapter{How to Read This Book}

This reference section of the book contains a summary of its six central claims, a diagram connecting them, and a glossary for reference.

\textbf{First time here?} Read the Preface and continue into \magevalue{chapter:problem}. You can skip the remaining front matter until its terminology becomes useful.

\textbf{Looking for a refresher?} Use \emph{What This Book Argues} for the six central claims and their relationships, and the Glossary for definitions.

The book asks how we can safely grant autonomy to commodity intelligence. \magevalue{chapter:problem} frames the engineering problem. Chapters \magevalue{chapter:modeling|num}--\magevalue{chapter:software-factories|num} develop MAGE through software engineering, explaining its mechanisms and examining the evidence. \magevalue{chapter:theory} develops the theory, \magevalue{chapter:profession} considers its broader implications, and the Conclusion returns to the opening question through responsibility. \autoref{book-map} traces this progression.
