\textbf{A practical reference for beginning, operating, and evaluating a Governed Engineering Environment}

Begin with one engineering surface: a recurring kind of change, an operational workflow, or a consequential obligation. This appendix follows that surface from the first investment through brownfield migration, delegated work, and measurement. Use the section that answers the decision in front of you. The \href{appendix-handbook.html}{MAGE Engineering Handbook} supplies concrete models and compositions of mechanisms; the main chapters develop the method behind them.

The governing question is whether the environment makes future work more capable, trustworthy, or economical than it costs to maintain.
