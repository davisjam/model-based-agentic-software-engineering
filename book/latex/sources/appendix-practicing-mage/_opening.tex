This appendix provides practical guidance for adopting and operating MAGE. It addresses four questions:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{\href{appendix-b-getting-started.html}{Getting Started (B.1)}:} Where should you begin, how should you choose a modeling approach, and how much engineering infrastructure should you build?
\item \textbf{\href{appendix-b-brownfield-migration.html}{Brownfield Migration (B.2)}:} How can you recover and reconcile models of an existing system, then introduce enforcement without rebuilding everything?
\item \textbf{\href{appendix-b-operating-a-gee.html}{Operating a GEE (B.3)}:} How do you organize delegated engineering work, evaluate its results, and recover from failures?
\item \textbf{\href{appendix-b-measuring-mage.html}{Measuring MAGE (B.4)}:} How can you determine whether the investment in models and controls is improving engineering outcomes?
\end{itemize}

The preceding chapters develop the principles of MAGE; \href{appendix-handbook.html}{Appendix A} provides detailed engineering blueprints. The guidance here concerns putting those ideas into practice.
