This appendix provides practical guidance for adopting and operating MAGE. It addresses four questions:

- **[Getting Started (B.1)](appendix-b-getting-started.html):** Where should you begin, how should you choose a modeling approach, and how much engineering infrastructure should you build?
- **[Brownfield Migration (B.2)](appendix-b-brownfield-migration.html):** How can you recover and reconcile models of an existing system, then introduce enforcement without rebuilding everything?
- **[Operating a GEE (B.3)](appendix-b-operating-a-gee.html):** How do you organize delegated engineering work, evaluate its results, and recover from failures?
- **[Measuring MAGE (B.4)](appendix-b-measuring-mage.html):** How can you determine whether the investment in models and controls is improving engineering outcomes?

The preceding chapters develop the principles of MAGE; [Appendix A](appendix-handbook.html) provides detailed engineering blueprints. The guidance here concerns putting those ideas into practice.
