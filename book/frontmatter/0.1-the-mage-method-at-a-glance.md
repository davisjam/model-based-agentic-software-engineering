# The MAGE Method at a Glance

MAGE is one method organized around two theses and a conversion loop. [ref:mage-method] shows the argument in one view: the old engineering problem created by scale, the changed economics created by commodity intelligence, and the method that lets engineering effort compound rather than dissipate into churn.

<!-- label: mage-method -->
<!-- figure: assets/mage-method.svg | The MAGE method. Scale creates the enduring reasoning problem; commodity intelligence changes its economics. Modeling makes engineering knowledge explicit and exposes richer semantic surfaces that extend what Alignment can govern — but Alignment does not depend on it: a direct, local path (tests, sandboxes, permissions, reviews) reaches Alignment without explicit models, at narrower reach. Governance conversion adapts recurring failures into durable models and controls, so engineering effort compounds as capital rather than dissipating into churn. -->

The Alignment Thesis fills the environment with governance mechanisms of four archetypes, each named for the role it plays rather than the outcome it serves. [ref:mechanism-archetypes] names the four and the role that distinguishes each.

<!-- label: mechanism-archetypes -->
| Mechanism | Role |
|---|---|
| **Constraint** | restricts actions |
| **Sensor** | observes violations |
| **Validator** | evaluates evidence |
| **Gate** | controls admission |
