# The MAGE Method at a Glance

MAGE is one method organized around two principles and a conversion loop. [ref:mage-method] shows the argument in one view: the old engineering problem created by scale, the changed economics created by commodity intelligence, and the method that lets engineering effort compound rather than dissipate into churn.

<!-- label: mage-method -->
<!-- figure: assets/mage-method.svg | The MAGE method. Scale creates the enduring reasoning problem; commodity intelligence changes its economics. Modeling makes consequential engineering knowledge explicit, expanding the semantic surface over which Alignment can give obligations authority. Governance conversion turns recurring failures and judgment into durable models and controls, allowing engineering effort to accumulate as capital rather than dissipate into churn. -->

Alignment acts through governance mechanisms of four archetypes, each named for the role it plays rather than the outcome it serves. [ref:mechanism-archetypes] names the four and the role that distinguishes each.

<!-- label: mechanism-archetypes -->
| Mechanism | Role |
|---|---|
| **Constraint** | restricts actions |
| **Sensor** | observes violations |
| **Validator** | evaluates evidence |
| **Gate** | controls admission |
