# How to Read This Book

The book asks one question in the Preface and answers it in the Conclusion. Everything between builds the
answer. The Preface poses it — *how do we safely grant autonomy to commodity intelligence?* — and the
Conclusion returns to it, gives the compact answer, and asks the converse: *what cannot be delegated?* Read
straight through and the argument unfolds in that order.

The Parts follow that sequence. Part I introduces the new engineering problem: it establishes the
changed economics and explains why the question has become urgent when implementation turns abundant relative
to judgment. Part II develops **Modeling** — how to choose purposeful representations that let finite
reasoners work over larger system properties. Part III develops **Alignment** — how constraints, sensors,
validators, and gates give engineering obligations authority. Part IV joins the two into a working method.
Part V is the empirical examination: first the originating case in depth, then independent industrial
practice in breadth. Part VI is the theoretical synthesis: it develops the theory, says where it should and
should not hold, identifies predictions that can be tested, and follows the implications for software
engineering and the engineer. The **Conclusion** then answers the Preface and lands on what stays the
engineer's responsibility. [ref:book-map] shows the progression.

Three shorter routes serve different purposes:

- **Do it Monday.** Part I → Part IV → Appendix Part I: Practice. Start with the premise, move directly to
  the working method, then use the practice appendices as a reference while applying it.
- **Show me the data.** Part I → Part V → Appendix Part II: Evidence. Read the framing, examine the book's
  case in practice, then inspect the evidence on which its broader claims rest.
- **I want the theory.** Part I → Part II → Part III → Part VI → the Conclusion. Follow the argument from the
  changed engineering substrate through modeling and alignment to its implications for software engineering.

Read straight through and the argument builds in order. Afterward, Parts II–IV can be revisited for the
method itself. The appendices are organized in two parts: Practice and Evidence. Neither is meant to be read
straight through. Appendix Part I is a working reference for applying and extending MAGE; Appendix Part II
collects the evidence behind the book's empirical claims. Start with the part — and then the appendix — that
answers the question in front of you. The Colophon at the end shows how the book itself was maintained as a
modeled, governed artifact. It comes after the argument rather than forming part of it.
