# How to read this book

The six Parts follow the method's argument in a deliberate order. Part I establishes the changed economics and the engineering problems that remain when implementation becomes abundant. Part II develops Modeling: how to choose purposeful representations that let finite reasoners work over larger system properties. Part III develops Alignment: how constraints, sensors, validators, and gates give engineering obligations authority. Modeling comes first because MAGE treats explicit abstraction as the principled way to scale both reasoning and governance; Alignment can exist without it, but its reach is narrower and human judgment must often bridge what the environment cannot represent. Part IV joins the two into a working method. Part V opens DocAble end to end and shows where that method came from. Part VI turns outward to independently evolved systems and asks what survives those comparisons as theory. [ref:book-map] shows that progression.

Three shorter routes serve different purposes:

- **Do it Monday:** Part I → Part IV → the appendices.
- **Convince me first:** Part I → Part V → Part VI.
- **I want the theory:** Part I → Part II → Part III → Part VI.

Read straight through and the argument builds in order. Afterward, Parts II–IV and the appendices are intended to work as a reference while you build.
