# How to read this book

The book asks one question in the Preface and answers it in the Conclusion. Everything between builds the
answer. The Preface poses it — *how do we safely grant autonomy to commodity intelligence?* — and the
Conclusion returns to it, gives the compact answer, and asks the converse: *what cannot be delegated?* Read
straight through and the argument unfolds in that order.

The Parts follow that sequence. Part I introduces the new engineering problem: it establishes the
changed economics and explains why the question has become urgent when implementation turns abundant relative
to judgment. Part II develops **Modeling** — how to choose purposeful representations that let finite
reasoners work over larger system properties. Part III develops **Alignment** — how constraints, sensors,
validators, and gates give engineering obligations authority. Modeling comes first because explicit
abstraction can scale reasoning and enlarge the semantic surface over which Alignment can operate. Alignment can act on
locally encoded obligations without separately externalizing them, but explicit models make richer semantics
available to both reasoning and control. Part IV joins the two into a working method. Part V supplies the evidence: first the originating case in
depth, then independent industrial practice in breadth. Part VI asks what the evidence means: it develops
the theory, says where it should and should not hold, identifies predictions that can be tested, and follows
the implications for software engineering and the engineer. The **Conclusion** then answers the Preface
and lands on what stays the engineer's responsibility. [ref:book-map] shows the progression.

Three shorter routes serve different purposes:

- **Do it Monday:** Part I → Part IV → Appendices A–E.
- **Convince me first:** Part I → Part V → Part VI → the Conclusion.
- **I want the theory:** Part I → Part II → Part III → Part VI → the Conclusion.

Read straight through and the argument builds in order. Afterward, Parts II–IV and the working appendices
can be revisited as reference material; the final appendices preserve the external cases and measurements
behind the argument. The Colophon at the end shows how the book itself was maintained as a modeled, governed
artifact. It comes after the argument rather than forming part of it.
