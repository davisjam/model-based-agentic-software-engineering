# How to read this book

The book asks one question in the Preface and answers it in the Conclusion. Everything between builds the
answer. The Preface poses it — *how do we safely grant autonomy to commodity intelligence?* — and the
Conclusion returns to it, gives the compact answer, and asks the converse: *what cannot be delegated?* Read
straight through and the argument arrives in that deliberate order.

The Parts follow the argument in sequence. Part I introduces the new engineering problem: it establishes the
changed economics and explains why the question has become urgent when implementation turns abundant relative
to judgment. Part II develops **Modeling** — how to choose purposeful representations that let finite
reasoners work over larger system properties. Part III develops **Alignment** — how constraints, sensors,
validators, and gates give engineering obligations authority. Modeling comes first because MAGE treats
explicit abstraction as the principled way to scale both reasoning and governance; Alignment can exist
without it, but its reach is narrower, and human judgment must often bridge what the environment cannot
represent. Part IV joins the two into a working method. Part V opens DocAble end to end and shows where that
method came from. Part VI turns outward — to independently evolved systems, to the profession, and to what
remains empirically open. The **Conclusion** then answers the Preface and lands on what stays the engineer's
responsibility. [ref:book-map] shows the progression.

Three shorter routes serve different purposes:

- **Do it Monday:** Part I → Part IV → Appendices A–E.
- **Convince me first:** Part I → Part V → Part VI → the Conclusion.
- **I want the theory:** Part I → Part II → Part III → Part VI → the Conclusion.

Read straight through and the argument builds in order. Afterward, Parts II–IV and the working appendices
can be revisited as reference material; the final appendices preserve the external cases and empirical
receipts behind the argument. The Colophon at the very end is a short reflexive
demonstration — the book itself was maintained as a modeled, governed artifact — offered after the argument
is over, not as part of it.
