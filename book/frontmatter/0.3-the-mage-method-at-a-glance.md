# The MAGE Method at a Glance

MAGE has two principles and a conversion loop. [ref:mage-method] shows the whole argument: scale creates the old problem, commodity intelligence changes its economics, and MAGE turns repeated effort into structure that future work can reuse.

<!-- label: mage-method -->
<!-- figure: assets/mage-method.svg | The MAGE method. Scale creates the enduring reasoning problem; commodity intelligence changes its economics. Modeling makes important engineering knowledge explicit, so Alignment can govern richer properties. Governance conversion turns recurring failures and judgment into models and controls that future work can reuse, so engineering effort can accumulate instead of being paid for again. -->

Alignment uses four kinds of governance mechanism, named for what each one does. [ref:mechanism-archetypes] names the four and the role that distinguishes each.

<!-- label: mechanism-archetypes -->
| Mechanism | Role |
|---|---|
| **Constraint** | restricts actions |
| **Sensor** | produces evidence |
| **Validator** | evaluates whether evidence satisfies an obligation |
| **Gate** | controls admission |
