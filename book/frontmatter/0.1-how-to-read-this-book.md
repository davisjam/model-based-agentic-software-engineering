# How to Read This Book

This reference section of the book contains a summary of its six central claims, a diagram connecting them, and a glossary for reference.

**First time here?** Read the Preface and continue into {{chapter:problem}}. You can skip the remaining front matter until its terminology becomes useful.

**Looking for a refresher?** Use *What This Book Argues* for the six central claims and their relationships, and the Glossary for definitions.

The book asks how we can safely grant autonomy to commodity intelligence. {{chapter:problem}} frames the engineering problem. Chapters {{chapter:modeling|num}}–{{chapter:software-factories|num}} develop MAGE through software engineering, explaining its mechanisms and examining the evidence. {{chapter:theory}} develops the theory, {{chapter:profession}} considers its broader implications, and the Conclusion returns to the opening question through responsibility. [ref:book-map] traces this progression.
