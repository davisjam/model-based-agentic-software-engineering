*Where is the next high-value gap?*

Brownfield progress has two independent dimensions. **Representation reach** measures how much of the
important system can be reasoned about through trustworthy externalized structure. **Authority reach**
measures how much of the important obligation surface is constrained, sensed, validated, or gated at an
appropriate boundary. Neither is a maturity score or a quantity that must reach 100 percent.

Strong local authority can exist with thin explicit modeling when tests, permissions, sandboxes, or
admission controls directly observe the relevant property. Rich models can also coexist with weak authority
when violations still depend on human detection. Choose the next investment according to the value of the
uncovered gap.

Representation reach and authority reach are independent on this axis: local controls can provide authority without rich
models, and modeled surfaces need not all receive authority.

Apply Audit → Synchronize → Govern → Extend to a selected surface rather than to the organization as a
whole. The Missing-Model Metric measures representation reach over an exercised surface, not authority
reach. Appendix G records the observed DocAble measurements.
