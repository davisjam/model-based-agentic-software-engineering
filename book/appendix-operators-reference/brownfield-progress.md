*Where is the next high-value gap?*

Brownfield progress has two independent dimensions. **Representation reach** asks how much of the
important system can be understood through trustworthy explicit models. **Enforcement reach**
asks how much of the important obligation surface is constrained, sensed, validated, or gated at an
appropriate boundary. Neither is a maturity score that must reach 100 percent.

Strong local enforcement can exist with thin explicit modeling when tests, permissions, sandboxes, or
admission controls directly observe the relevant property. Rich models can also coexist with weak enforcement
when violations still depend on human detection. Address whichever uncovered gap matters most.
