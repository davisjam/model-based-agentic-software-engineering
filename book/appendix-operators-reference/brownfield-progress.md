*Where is the next high-value gap?*

Brownfield progress has two independent dimensions. **Representation reach** asks how much of the important
system can be reasoned about through trustworthy externalized structure. **Authority reach** asks how much
of the important obligation surface is constrained, sensed, validated, or gated at an appropriate boundary.
Neither is a maturity score, and neither must reach 100 percent.

A system may have strong local authority with thin explicit modeling: tests, permissions, sandboxes, and
admission controls can govern what they can observe directly. It may also have rich models with weak
authority — the organization understands the system but still depends on people to notice violations. The
next investment goes where the mismatch is valuable enough to matter.

The diagonal is not a ladder: a sandbox governs what it can observe whether or not a model describes it. You
do not have to model before you can align, and you do not have to align everything you have modeled.

Use the Audit → Synchronize → Govern → Extend track to move a chosen surface, not the whole organization. The
Missing-Model Metric measures representation reach over an exercised surface, not governance coverage — a
sandbox may supply real authority without moving it. Appendix H carries the observed drain.
