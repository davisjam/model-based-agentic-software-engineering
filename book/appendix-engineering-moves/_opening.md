This appendix shows how the same engineering judgment can produce different mechanisms in different systems.

Each section starts from a recurring engineering problem, names the move that addresses it, and shows two realizations. The examples expose the shared engineering structure behind otherwise different mechanisms.

Read each page in one direction:

**Problem → Move → two realizations.**

A move is transferable engineering judgment: close an action surface, derive obligations from a model, deliver policy where a decision occurs. A mechanism is one concrete realization of that judgment: a typed mutation API, a coverage census, a context-injection hook. Mechanisms vary by system; the move is portable.

Appendix A shows how mechanisms compose into engineering capabilities. This appendix shows how one move can take different forms. The online MAGE Mechanism Catalog contains the broader implementation repertoire.
