### Zenseact

<!-- label: field-guide-zenseact -->
<!-- figure: assets/field-guide-zenseact.svg | *Zenseact.* A split-org team: a platform team owns the safety substrate, domain teams own the judgment — the Reasoning-Horizon door. -->

**What they are.** An autonomous-driving software organization. Public reports describe a platform-team / domain-team split with federated skills.

**Where they enter.** Through context and delegation. Zenseact invests in what enters an agent's reasoning and who may act, and lands on the **Reasoning Horizon**.

**The MAGE reading.** Zenseact draws the line outright: centralize the mechanisms, decentralize the judgment. A platform team owns authentication, sessions, execution, and safety. Domain teams own their own agents, tools, and instructions. A single team building every agent would become a coordination bottleneck; a shared substrate with distributed ownership scales with the organization instead. Each domain team encodes its expertise once, for every agent that later touches the domain, so context routing, permission, and delegation all grow dense where the domain knowledge already lives.

**What you take away.** Build the common substrate once; leave the domain judgment where the domain knowledge already lives.
