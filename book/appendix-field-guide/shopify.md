### Shopify

*Commerce · shared organizational context for agent work*

#### Verso — Evidence

**What the public record shows.** Shopify's public account describes agent work occurring in shared, observable channels rather than disappearing into private sessions. The record gives a one-month snapshot of **59,918 sessions across 5,170 channels involving more than 7,000 people**, together with the characterization of the system as "multiplayer by construction."[^shopify-record] Shared sessions become searchable organizational artifacts; repeated knowledge can then be extracted into skills, conventions, and defaults that later agents can retrieve. This is a soft form of externalization: retrievable prose and skills rather than a structured executable system model.

**Boundary of the evidence.** The evidence does not establish the full causal chain `session → skill → engineering capital`. It establishes observability and mechanisms for reuse; MAGE supplies the further interpretation that recurring judgment extracted into reusable skills constitutes governance conversion and may become engineering capital.

**Portable lesson.** Experience cannot compound organizationally while it stays private. Make work observable before trying to make its lessons reusable.

[^shopify-record]: The session/channel/participant snapshot and the "multiplayer by construction" characterization are from Shopify's public account: Javier Moreno and Burke Libbey, "Under the River," Shopify Engineering, May 28, 2026, https://shopify.engineering/under-the-river.

#### Recto — MAGE Interpretation

**MAGE reading.** Shopify sharpens a condition on engineering capital: the experience has to become observable before it can become durable. A private session does not automatically become an organizational artifact. A shared session becomes an organizational artifact that can be searched, corrected, mined, and — where the lesson deserves reuse — converted into a skill or default. This sits at the knowledge end of Modeling, not the executable-system-model end.

**Interpretive boundary.** The mapping treats observability as the precondition for compounding; it does not license reading any particular observed session as already converted into engineering capital.
