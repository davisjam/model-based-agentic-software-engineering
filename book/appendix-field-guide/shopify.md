### Shopify

<!-- label: field-guide-shopify -->
<!-- figure: assets/field-guide-shopify.svg | *Shopify.* A shared-environment team: agent work done in public becomes compounding capital — the Engineering-Environment door. -->

**What they are.** A commerce platform. Public reports give hard monthly numbers for org-wide agent use.

**Where they enter.** Through the shared environment. Shopify invests in organizational knowledge as an environmental artifact, and lands on the **Engineering Environment**.

**The MAGE reading.** Shopify runs its agent in public Slack channels, so private learning becomes a searchable, minable artifact. One engineer starts a task, another enters the thread with a correction, and the transcript stays for the next engineer: **59,918 sessions across 5,170 channels touching more than 7,000 people in a single month**, agents "multiplayer by construction." Each new agent is a profile over shared infrastructure, not a bespoke platform, and routine structure factors into deterministic stages. 6.6 reads Shopify as the corpus's strongest instance of experience compounding, and keeps one refinement: experience must become *observable* before it can compound. A private session does not compound; a shared one can be mined into a skill.

**What you take away.** A private session does not compound; a shared, observable one becomes a skill every later agent inherits.
