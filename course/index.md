Teach software engineering as the discipline of governing capable, autonomous agents.

This companion provides reusable materials for teaching software engineering using MAGE (Model-Based Agentic Software Engineering). The method is developed in full in the **[MAGE textbook](https://davisjam.github.io/model-based-agentic-software-engineering/book/)**.

The curriculum — the **[Lectures](lectures/index.md)**, the **[Semester Project](project/index.md)**, and the **[Oral Exams](oral-exams.md)** — is organized by its intellectual structure, independent of any calendar. The **[Reference Course](reference-course/index.md)** shows one demonstrated way to schedule and teach it: ECE 30861 — Software Engineering, Purdue University, Fall 2026.

The Semester Project gives student teams a substantial problem through which to practice the concepts across the software lifecycle.
