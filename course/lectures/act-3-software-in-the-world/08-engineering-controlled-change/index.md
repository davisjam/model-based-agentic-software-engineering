*How do these technical concerns become a sustained engineering practice?*

The course began by treating software engineering as informed control over consequential software systems. Agents change how much realization work can be delegated, but they do not eliminate the need for judgment about what must remain true. Sustained engineering capability depends on the software an organization possesses and on its ability to change that software while preserving consequential properties.

**Lecture: Course in Review — Engineering Controlled Change.** The concluding lecture connects the technical subjects through the engineering models and controls they require. Deployment topologies, database schemas, dependency graphs, workload models, threat models, and operational objectives answer different questions about the same software system. They can also disagree or become stale as the implementation changes. Engineers must decide which representations to maintain, what evidence to demand, and which obligations justify enforcement.
