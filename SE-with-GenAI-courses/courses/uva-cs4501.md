# UVA CS 4501 — Software Engineering and LLMs

Fall 2025 · Sebastian Elbaum · undergraduate practicum ·
[evidence](../sources/uva-cs4501-se-llms.md)

## What it teaches

"Students will learn to leverage LLMs to enhance their productivity across the software
development lifecycle, from coding and testing to debugging and maintenance," with attention to
"ethical and safety implications." The published week sequence walks the conventional lifecycle in
order — enhanced coding → tooling and customer discovery → "Requirements, user stories, and specs"
→ "Architectures and design analysis" → robustness → debugging/maintenance → front ends and
agentic development → MVP — converging on a team-built MVP. Method: "Alternate short lectures and
activities (vibing, interview, analysis, ...), emphasis on experiencing a new type of development."

## How it is organized (interpretation)

An SDLC-shaped experiential practicum: the lifecycle is the spine, LLMs are threaded through every
stage. One visible design nuance: the AI-use policy modulates per assignment ("some assignments
will have explicit requirements about not using AI or how to use it"), suggesting deliberate
contrast between assisted and unassisted work.

## Evidence caveat

Moderate: primary syllabus with verbatim description, schedule, grading, and policies — but
readings and all assignment specs are behind Canvas, so per-concept treatment is largely
unobservable.

## Adoptability (drop-in availability, audited 2026-09-30)

**Category 3 — inspectable but not adoptable.** The entire instructional package is routed through
Canvas by explicit policy; a syllabus and a topic-title week list are what remains.

- **Public:** a single self-contained syllabus page — meeting time and place, instructor and TA
  contacts, grading weights (attendance 20% / 5–6 assignments 50% / team project 30%), the full
  letter-grade scale, and a late policy · a 15-week week-by-week **topic** table, titles only.
- **Absent:** **zero** slides, assignments, handouts, notebooks, or repos. A full `href` inventory of
  both the F25 and F26 course pages found no link to any such artifact — only university policy
  pages, the lab site, mailto addresses, and CDN assets. The page states it outright: *"Slides and
  assignments available through Canvas."* No reading list ("materials provided by the instructor via
  Canvas"). No rubrics beyond weights and the grade scale. No project brief for the 30% component.
- **Login:** yes, for effectively all instructional content — slides, assignments, and readings are
  stated to live on Canvas behind UVA NetBadge.
- **License:** **no license stated.** Neither course page nor the instructor homepage carries a
  copyright, CC mark, or reuse statement.
- **Checked:** the instructor homepage, `files/seLLMs.html` (F25), and `files/seLLMs2026.html` (F26,
  found by link extraction) — all 200, with the full anchor inventory extracted from both.
