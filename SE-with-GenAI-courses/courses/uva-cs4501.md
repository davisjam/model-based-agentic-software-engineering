# UVA CS 4501 — Software Engineering and LLMs

Fall 2025 · Sebastian Elbaum · undergraduate practicum ·
[evidence](../sources/uva-cs4501-se-llms.md)

## What it teaches

"Students will learn to leverage LLMs to enhance their productivity across the software
development lifecycle, from coding and testing to debugging and maintenance," with attention to
"ethical and safety implications." The published week sequence walks the conventional lifecycle in
order — enhanced coding → tooling and customer discovery → "Requirements, user stories, and specs"
→ "Architectures and design analysis" → robustness → debugging/maintenance → front ends and
agentic development → MVP — converging on a team-built MVP. Method: "Alternate short lectures and
activities (vibing, interview, analysis, ...), emphasis on experiencing a new type of development."

## How it is organized (interpretation)

An SDLC-shaped experiential practicum: the lifecycle is the spine, LLMs are threaded through every
stage. One visible design nuance: the AI-use policy modulates per assignment ("some assignments
will have explicit requirements about not using AI or how to use it"), suggesting deliberate
contrast between assisted and unassisted work.
