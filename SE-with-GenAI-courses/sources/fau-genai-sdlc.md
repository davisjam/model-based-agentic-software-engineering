## A. Explicit course content (quoted / cited)

### Identity
- University: Florida Atlantic University; department: EECS (College of Engineering and Computer Science)
- Number/title: COT 6930 (Topics in Computer Science), section title "Generative AI Software Development Lifecycles" (the EECS electives page renders it "Generative AI and Software Development liFECYCLES" [sic]); Spring 2026 section 001; Fall 2025 section 004
- Term: offered Fall 2024, Fall 2025, Spring 2026 (Spring 2026 per Geng et al. Table 1 and the Spring 2026 SimpleSyllabus URL)
- Instructor: Fernando Koch (Industry Professor; leads The Generative Intelligence Lab at FAU)
- Level: graduate ("Prerequisite: Graduate Standing")
- Format: lecture + labs; Fall 2025 meeting time "Tuesday and Thursday from 2:00 PM - 3:20 PM" (search-index fragment); Fall 2024 "Tuesdays and Thursdays, 12:30–1:50 PM" (electives page)
