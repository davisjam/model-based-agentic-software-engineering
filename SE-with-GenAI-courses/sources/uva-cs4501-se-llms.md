### Stated learning objectives
- No enumerated objectives section ("None explicitly stated in the syllabus" per full-page extraction). Course description carries the intent (verbatim): "This course will explore the transformative impact of Large Language Models (LLMs) on modern software engineering. Students will learn to leverage LLMs to enhance their productivity across the software development lifecycle, from coding and testing to debugging and maintenance. We will also practice how to be cognizant of the ethical and safety implications of using LLMs in software development."
- Teaching method (verbatim): "Alternate short lectures and activities (vibing, interview, analysis, ...), emphasis on experiencing a new type of development."
