## A. Explicit course content (quoted / cited)

### Identity
University of Chicago · Masters Program in Computer Science (MPCS), Dept. of Computer Science · MPCS 51238 · "Design, Build, Ship" · Spring 2026: two sections, MPCS 51238-1 (Tue 5:30–8:30pm) and 51238-2 (Wed 5:30–8:30pm), Crerar 298 · Instructor: Andre Fiuza Marques · graduate (MPCS elective, 100 units) · prerequisite: one of MPCS 51036 / 51040 / 51042 / 51046 / 51100 or CAPP 30122 (i.e., an MPCS intro-programming course). The course also ran in Summer 2025 (program article) and appears again in Autumn 2026 listings.

### Stated learning objectives
From the official listing (search-mediated): "Transform ideas into launched products and services with real users in 10 weeks." "This hands-on course focuses on iterative design and rapid software development, using AI-assisted tools to accelerate the entire process. Students will work individually or in small teams to build, launch, and acquire users for a single project they will work on across the quarter."
From the program article: the course aims to "give students the tools, confidence, and freedom to turn abstract ideas into tangible impact."

### Organizing sequence
Not evident in available materials (no public week-by-week schedule). ### Assignments and project structure
One quarter-long project per student or small team: "build, launch, and acquire users for a single project they will work on across the quarter" (official listing, search-mediated). Project domains: "civic, entrepreneurial, creative, educational, experimental, or social impact-oriented" (program article/search summary); "whether that means launching a startup, building a civic tool, or experimenting with cutting-edge AI" (program article). "It's very much a class that is what you want it to be and what you make of it" (program article). Grading and milestone structure: not evident in available materials.

## B. Surveyor notes (interpretation, labeled)
- Coverage gaps dominate: the official listing page could not be fetched directly (TLS misconfiguration for this tool; description recovered through search-engine renderings and cross-checked across two independent queries and the catalog), and no syllabus, schedule, assignments, grading, or tool list is public. Everything beyond the catalog paragraph rests on one program marketing article. A cautionary note: one search-engine summary attached the sentence "design, build, evaluate, and operate agentic AI systems" to this course; that sentence most likely belongs to a neighboring catalog entry (e.g., an agentic-AI course such as MPCS 51900) and could not be verified as 51238 text — it is deliberately NOT quoted in §A.
- Interpretation: the course is organized as a 10-week lean-startup-style shipping practicum — one project, launched early, iterated against real users — in which generative-AI development tools are the enabling accelerant ("less time on setup and boilerplate") rather than an object of study. It plausibly teaches engineering-with-AI by immersion, but the public record shows no explicit pedagogy about directing, verifying, or governing AI-produced work. Classified core comparator provisionally because AI-assisted development is named in the catalog description as central to the method; the classification should be revisited if a syllabus surfaces showing it is primarily an entrepreneurship course that merely permits AI tooling.
