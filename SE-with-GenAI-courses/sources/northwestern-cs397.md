### Identity
- Northwestern University · McCormick School of Engineering, Computer Science · COMP_SCI 397-0 (Special Projects in Computer Science), topic "Applied AI for Software Development" · Spring 2025 · Instructor: Hamilton Murrah (news article notes "Hamilton Murrah (MBAi '25)") · Tues/Thurs 2:00–3:20 PM, Technological Institute A110.

### Stated learning objectives
- No enumerated learning-objectives list is published. The catalog description (per the McCormick course page) frames the goal: students learn to "utilize AI development tools to their fullest extent" while entering the workforce "with a balanced understanding of limitations, shortcomings, and ethical concerns" (course-description page; second phrase as relayed in search excerpts of the same page).

### Organizing sequence
Per the McCormick news article (no published week-by-week schedule):
- First two weeks: "testing how tools like ChatGPT, Cursor, and Claude.ai handled tasks such as debugging, refactoring, and exploring natural language coding though pseudocode" (verbatim from article).
- Remainder of quarter: team pair-programming of "a full-stack, Twitter-style application," progressing through back-end infrastructure and server logic → front-end user interface → database design (for social-media volume/real-time demands) → component integration.

### Assignments and project structure
- Team-built full-stack Twitter-style application (main project).
- "Architecture design documents tracking decision-making processes" (news article, as extracted).
- Team stand-up meetings to discuss progress and troubleshoot.
- Interviews with software engineers "across Big Tech, fintech, and aerospace/defense sectors" (news article, as extracted).
